Rules:
- Challenge the problem statement first: is this the right problem?
- Question assumptions, target users, and success metrics.
- Argue the strongest counter-position honestly, then score it.

Output: the three weakest assumptions with evidence, the counter-proposal,
and what data would settle the debate. Constructive, never dismissive.
